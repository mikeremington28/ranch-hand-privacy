# Remington Acres Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** April 3, 2026

Remington Acres ("we," "us," or "our") publishes mobile applications and related services.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled for apps published by Remington Acres, including **Cold Bore** and **Ranch Hand**, unless a specific app provides a separate privacy policy.

## Information We Collect

Depending on the app and the features you choose to use, we may collect or process:

- Information you enter into the app
- App content such as logs, records, notes, settings, and photos
- Device and app information needed for app functionality or support
- Optional permission-based data such as location, microphone, camera, photos, files, or cloud backup data
- Purchase or subscription status information provided through Apple

## How We Use Information

We use information only to:

- Operate app features
- Save your settings and content
- Support backup, restore, import, and export features
- Provide optional subscription or paid functionality
- Respond to support requests
- Improve app stability and functionality
- Comply with legal obligations

## Data Sharing

We do **not** sell your personal information.

We may share information only when necessary to:

- provide platform-based services such as App Store purchases or device cloud services
- comply with law or legal process
- protect the rights, safety, or security of users or Remington Acres

## Data Storage

Depending on the app, data may be stored:

- locally on your device
- in your personal cloud storage or backup service, if you enable that feature
- through limited platform services required for purchases, subscriptions, backup, restore, or app delivery

## Your Choices

You may be able to:

- decline optional permissions such as location, microphone, camera, or photos
- disable app permissions in device settings
- delete app content from within the app
- remove the app from your device
- manage subscriptions through your Apple account

## Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed to children under 13 unless explicitly stated otherwise. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through direct support channels without appropriate consent.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted at this location, and the "Last updated" date will be revised.

Cold Bore may store app content such as shooting logs, session notes, timer data, entries, exports, and photos.

Cold Bore may request access to:
- **Location** for optional location-based session features
- **Microphone** for optional audio shot detection or timer-related features
- **Camera or Photos** for optional image capture or attachment
- **Cloud services such as iCloud** for optional backup and restore on supported devices

Cold Bore may also offer **subscriptions or in-app purchases** through Apple's App Store.
